Key investment committee challenges

Unverified purchase basis: No acquisition consideration or actual T12 NOI is disclosed publicly. Underwriting prices are analyst hypotheticals.
Clinical operating risk: Care staffing, complaints and licensing cannot be optimized purely as cost ratios.
Exit leverage: Stress cases can leave gross sale proceeds below outstanding mortgage principal.
Asset aggregation: 359-unit totals conceal individual community rent rolls, license scope and differences in local markets.
